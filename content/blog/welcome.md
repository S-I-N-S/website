# welcome to the reflux.cloud blog


We are excited to introduce the **reflux.cloud** blog. Our small team are passionate about FOSS, security, and all things tech.

## What We Do

reflux.cloud is our home for different projects we build in our areas of interest. All of our solutions are all trying to solve problems we have with the current market those solutions land in. Sometimes, the inspiration for our implementations are as simple as wondering how a piece of software is built. What better way to learn than to try and fail miserably. Today, we have two main projects:

### [GameDay](https://gameday.reflux.cloud)
Fast and easy-to-use sports stats and visualization platform. Currently focused on American Football.

### [Simple-Sandbox](https://reflux.cloud/simple-sandbox)
An interactive malware analysis sandbox for secure threat investigation.

## Our Goal

Learning everyday, building interesting solutions, and sharing our progress with the world.
