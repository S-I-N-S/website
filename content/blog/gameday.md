# GameDay Launch?


I have officially and subtly launched the first solution under the reflux.cloud banner. To an overwhelming round of silence, and maybe a pin dropping across the room. 

But, this was expected. When this project was started, it was to solve a specific problem I was having. It was not started with the primary focus being to make money. I encountered a problem in the real world. Which is typically a scary thing, but somehow I managed. The solution to this problem was what eventually became GameDay.

## But what is it?

GameDay is currently a stats logging web app for American Football. You can to log stats quickly during the game. Then have those stats visualized for mid-game or half-time adjustments. You can log unlimited amounts of games on the platform with all of the stats from those games confined to the game they were logged in.

## But why?

Other programs likely handle their limited staff differently than ours, but one of our priorities during games is to place a minimum of one coach in the box. Their job is to handle basic stats for what is going well and not, and to make call outs for different items we did or did not game planned for. 

I have been this coach the past seasons. Having played college ball, the assumption is that my 'Football IQ' is high enough to see things as they develop and make the appropriate callouts. This couldn't be farther from the truth. This should come as no surprise to anyone, but the person who got hit in the head for over 8 years isn't actually very bright. So instead of having to increase my intelligence I wanted to click bright buttons on a screen and let a computer do the hard work for me. Thus, GameDay was born. 

## Future plans

We have a few challenges lined in front of this becoming something that is leveraged by coaches. The main one is that it was designed to solved my problems before it was designed to sell. That means it is hard to market because it wasn't designed around a marketable feature or solving a wide spread problem. There are likely coaches out there who would like to use a web app like this, but the challenge is finding them and telling them about it.

Next steps for GameDay are tentative and still in the works. The current plans may change, but are as follows:
- Determine how to market and where the target audience is
- Expand to cover Basketball and Baseball stats
- Redesign for marketability

The launch of GameDay has already taught so many lessons that it has paid for itself 10x over. We will continue to build this up and see where it goes.
